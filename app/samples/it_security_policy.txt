Northwind Freight Pvt. Ltd. — IT Security Policy

Fictional demo data for DocuMind.

1. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Passwords are changed only when there is a reason to believe they were exposed; there is no forced periodic change.

2. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the travel portal and all remote access. SMS codes are not allowed; use the authenticator app.

3. Devices
Company laptops have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

4. Phishing and incidents
Report a suspected phishing email by forwarding it to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of noticing it, then delete it. Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours. Do not try to investigate an incident yourself.

5. Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer shipment data is Confidential. Restricted data (salary and health records) may only be stored in approved HR systems.

6. Software
Only software from the company software portal may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT helpdesk.
