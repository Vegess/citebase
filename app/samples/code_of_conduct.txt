Northwind Freight Pvt. Ltd. — Code of Conduct

Fictional demo data for Citebase.

1. Gifts and hospitality
Employees may accept gifts from customers or suppliers worth up to Rs 2,000 per occasion. Anything above that must be declined or declared to Compliance within 7 days. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts are never accepted.

2. Conflicts of interest
Declare any personal or financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor to Compliance before taking part in any related decision. Declarations are reviewed every year.

3. Respect at work
Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act, which completes its inquiry within 90 days.

4. Speaking up
Concerns can be reported to the ethics hotline at ethics@northwind.example, which accepts anonymous reports. Employees who raise concerns in good faith are protected from retaliation under the whistleblower policy.

5. Social media
Employees must not share confidential company or customer information on social media, and must not speak on behalf of the company without approval from Communications.
