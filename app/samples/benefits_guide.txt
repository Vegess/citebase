Northwind Freight Pvt. Ltd. — Benefits Guide 2026

Fictional demo data for DocuMind.

1. Health insurance
Every employee is covered by a family floater health insurance policy of Rs 5 lakh, covering the employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents can be added for an annual premium of Rs 9,000, deducted from salary.

2. Wellness
Employees can claim gym or fitness reimbursement of up to Rs 1,000 per month. A free annual health check-up is available at partner hospitals.

3. Learning
Each employee has a learning budget of Rs 30,000 per financial year for courses, certifications and books. Unused budget does not carry forward.

4. Provident fund and gratuity
The company contributes 12% of basic salary to the provident fund. Gratuity is paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act after 5 years of continuous service.

5. Employee referral
A successful referral earns Rs 25,000, paid after the new hire completes probation.
